The Budgets page verified an existing account-wide Actions budget of $0, with $0 spent and status `Usage blocked`, on November 12, 2026. Packages and Git LFS also have existing $0 blocked budgets. These are excess-spending controls; included usage remains subject to GitHub's allowance. Do not add a payment method, raise budgets, or enable overages. Recheck these controls before later hosted work if account settings change. If the free allowance is exhausted, keep checking locally and resume hosted runs when it resets.

CI uses one `ubuntu-latest` job, a two-minute timeout, cancellation of superseded runs, no matrix, no schedule, no artifacts, no cache, no packages, and no deployment. No paid organization, protected-branch upgrade, paid reviewer, Codespace, or new hosting account is needed. Timeouts limit consumption but do not enforce an account spending cap.
